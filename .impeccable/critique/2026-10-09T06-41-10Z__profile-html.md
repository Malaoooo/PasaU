## Design Specificity Verdict

Design review: looks like UniCycle (velvet glass cards, pills, lime/amber chips) but the structure is a generic marketplace account page. The unique mechanics are missing or buried: the 6-digit code, meetup spot and time appear nowhere; a reserved deal in History reads only "Swapped to A student · Reserved"; the waiting line hides behind a muted "Interested people" link; verification is section 5 of Settings, about two phone screens down; vouches are just a number.

Detector: CLI scan clean (0), because the Settings form is built by JavaScript at runtime. In-browser scan: 3 low-contrast placeholders on Settings at 3.4:1 (needs 4.5:1): "At least 8 characters" (#pw-1), "you@school.edu.ph" (#vr-email), "Student number or anything that helps us confirm" (#vr-note). A fourth, "Type your school's name" (#e-school-other), has the same colours but wasn't flagged. No false positives. Agreement: the design review independently flagged that instructions live in those placeholders. Source: inputs built in profile.js:400, 433, 491-492; only styled by app.css:362 with no ::placeholder rule.

Visual overlays: drawn in the [Human] tab in the desktop app's browser pane (Settings, phone width).

## Overall Impression
Warm copy and real accessibility groundwork, but it behaves like a settings app with a list attached. The biggest opportunity: make the profile answer "what do I need to do next?" (who's in line, the next meetup, what's unverified).

## What's Working
- Specific, human copy ("We'll tell you if the price drops", "They aren't told who reported them", "Your email is never shown").
- Accessibility basics: skip link, accessibility panel, visible focus, labelled status dropdowns, 44px tabs, no sideways scroll at 390px or 200% zoom.
- Status chips read instantly; "Move to next in line" is a genuinely good idea, just hidden.

## Priority Issues

[P1] Delete, Remove, Unblock and Log out are the strongest-looking buttons
- Why: .pill.ghost is only styled inside .sheet/.empty (app.css:36, 220), so on the profile they render solid (black in light mode; near Edit/Renew colour in dark). Eye and thumb go to the riskiest button first.
- Fix: style .p-panel/.p-head .pill.ghost as outline; armed Delete in --err; Delete last or behind "More".
- Command: /impeccable polish

[P1] Irreversible actions use three confirm patterns, some none
- Why: Delete arms forever, never names the listing or the reserver (profile.js:234-238); Sold needs picking twice in a dropdown that silently reverts, armed flag never clears (249-255); Looking For Remove, Saved Remove and Unblock are instant (300, 319, 512).
- Fix: one inline confirm ("Delete 'Scientific Calculator fx-991ES'? 1 person has it reserved. [Delete] [Keep]") that disarms after ~5 s; a real "Mark as sold" button with the same confirm; Undo toast for low-stakes removals; route Reserved→Available through release_reservation.
- Command: /impeccable harden

[P1] The page ignores the core loop (handovers, line, trust)
- Why: this is where students come to see what to do next; it's what makes the page generic.
- Fix: "Next handover" card above tabs when a deal is reserved or has a meetup (spot · time · Open chat · Show code); "N in line" on each listing row; "Get verified" chip beside the name; "In progress" separate from finished History (profile.js:349-353).
- Command: /impeccable shape

[P2] Failed loads look like empty states
- Why: tabWanted 283-305, tabSaved 310-323, tabVouches 366-378, blocked list 506-508, verification 487-489 never check r.error; a failed verification check re-shows the form and invites duplicates.
- Fix: "Couldn't load …" + Retry, like tabListings/tabHistory; plain-language errors.
- Command: /impeccable harden

[P2] Settings is one ~2,000px wall
- Why: 7 sections, 22 controls, 3 save models; no <form>, so Enter does nothing and password managers can't detect the password form; header not refreshed after save (profile.js:426); instructions in low-contrast placeholders.
- Fix: group (Profile / Account & security / Notifications & privacy / Verification) with <details> or an in-panel index; real forms; renderHead after save; visible hint text + ::placeholder colour; "Changes save automatically" labels.
- Command: /impeccable layout
